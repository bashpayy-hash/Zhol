# 5. Where to find the right people

Target: 200 qualified contacts in 2 weeks. Qualify before contacting: **6–100 trucks + weekly lanes abroad + province in the beachhead**.

| Source | How | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Albo Nazionale degli Autotrasportatori** (public register search) | Search by province (VR, BS, BG, MI, MN, VI, PD, TV, TN, BZ, MO, RE, TO, CN) | Company name, registration; then find the website or phone |
| **Google Maps** | "autotrasporti internazionali" / "trasporti internazionali Germania" / "spedizioni Austria" + city | Phone, website, reviews (size hint) |
| **Company websites** | Look for "linee regolari Germania/Austria", "groupage", "camion frigo", number of vehicles, "ufficio traffico" contacts | Qualification + direct office number |
| **LinkedIn** | Titles: "responsabile traffico", "ufficio traffico", "traffic manager", "dispatcher", "titolare" + "autotrasporti" + province | Named people at the office |
| **Trade fairs** | Exhibitor lists of LetExpo (Verona), Transpotec Logitec (Milan), transport logistic (Munich) | Companies investing in growth |
| **Associations** | Regional offices of FAI, CNA Fita, Confartigianato Trasporti; Fedespedi member directory (forwarders) | Warm introductions (see `02-associations-and-partners.md`) |
| **Freight-exchange profiles** | Public company profiles on TIMOCOM / Trans.eu show lanes and fleet | Lanes abroad confirmation |
| **Customers' side** | Fresh-produce markets (e.g. Vittoria), food exporters, automotive suppliers: "who carries your goods to Germany?" | Names of hauliers on the exact lanes |
| **Drivers' and hauliers' groups** | Public Facebook and Telegram groups of Italian *autotrasportatori* (read and learn; post only where rules allow) | Live pain, vocabulary, names |
| **TEF / Bocconi** | TEF introductions; Bocconi alumni in logistics | Warm intros |

**Qualification checklist (before the first message):**
- [ ] 6–100 vehicles (website, photos, Albo bracket)
- [ ] Weekly lanes to DE / AT / CH / FR / PL (website or freight-exchange profile)
- [ ] In a beachhead province
- [ ] A named person (owner or traffic office) and a channel (mobile/WhatsApp, email, LinkedIn)

Track everything in `leads_tracker.csv`, one row per company.
Status flow: `new → contacted → replied → interviewed → loads_shared → pilot → paying | lost(reason)`.
