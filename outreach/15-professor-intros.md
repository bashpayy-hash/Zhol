# 15. Bocconi professors: one introduction each, to a named company

Cold letters to companies get few answers (owner, 12 Nov 2026: «может есть прям компания с которой могут познакомить
профессора боккони? При колд имейлинге они что то не отвечают»). A professor who knows a company can ask one person
to give us twenty minutes, and that person answers. So each letter below asks one professor for one introduction
and nothing else. The letter carries a short Italian paragraph the professor can paste or forward as it is.

**Who first.** These professors had our survey letter. Their `?src=` tags already brought answers: De Giovanni 2,
Baccelli 1, Senn 1, Grando 1, Zamboni 1, Gallmann 1. Veronesi has not answered yet, but she has the most direct
line to a company with its own traffic office. Send Veronesi and Baccelli first, Senn and De Giovanni a day later.

**How to send.** From the @studbocconi.it address, as before. Addresses are in `12-bocconi-professors.md`
(Baccelli's is public; the others through the Bocconi address book). One letter a professor, Monday to Thursday
morning. No reminder for an introduction: if a professor does not answer in a week, leave it.

**After a yes.** Write to the person the same day. The first line names the professor
(«Ci ha suggerito di scriverle la Prof.ssa Veronesi…»). Log each contact in `leads_tracker.csv`.

The links carry their own `?src=intro-…` tags, so we see which introduction brought an answer.

my name is Alikhan Bashpay, a Bocconi student. My team and I are building zhol, an AI platform that estimates the risk of bans, closures and strikes for each shipment, by road, rail or sea, warns the people who plan it in advance and updates them in real time.
