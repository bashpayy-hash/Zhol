Dear Professor Grando,

thank you again for sharing our survey. As a follow-up, I attach the results and a short update on zhol, an AI
platform that estimates whether a shipment, by road, rail or sea, risks a ban, a closure or a strike and what it would
cost, warns the people who plan it in advance and updates them in real time as things change.

In short, 8 of 9 people who know the field see shipments abroad change often, a stopped shipment costs the carrier
€300–1,000, and €5–10 a vehicle a month is a fair price. Our radar already caught real events this month and is open to
everyone at https://zhol.live; the beta for traffic offices is on the way. Reaching the companies themselves is hard,
though: only 2 replied to our letters.

If you know anyone in the field, could you forward them this message or the link to our site, or share the contact of
someone we could write to? We would of course tell them how we found them. Any advice is welcome too.

Kind regards,
zhol team
