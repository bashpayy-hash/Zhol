Question 4A clear GTM strategy

Start where restrictions hurt most, a day trip from Milan

  • ≈2,350 offices in the North to start with: a day trip from Milan, sold in Italian
  • 92 companies and 87 people already named from public sources
  • 4 pilots of four weeks, measured against the office’s own process
Who paysOwners of hauliers with 6–100 trucks and weekly lanes to DE, AT, CH, FR or PL, mostly over the Brenner. The dispatcher who plans tomorrow is the daily user.
Where firstLombardy, Veneto, Emilia-Romagna, Piemonte and Trentino-Alto Adige: about 2,350 offices. Then the South–North long haul hit by blockades, then Austrian, German and Slovenian hauliers on Italian lanes.
HowFounder-led, in Italian. A short WhatsApp or call, a 30-minute visit to review one recent disruption, then a free pilot with a written success criterion.
Already done92 companies in the starting provinces and 87 named people to interview (owners, traffic managers, forwarders, associations), from public sources. Personal first messages drafted in Italian.
