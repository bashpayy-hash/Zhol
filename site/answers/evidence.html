Targets set at Workshop 1. At Workshop 2, on 13 November 2026, we had 10 survey answers and no interview yet: cold letters got no reply, so the interviews now come through introductions.

Breadth as well as depth: seven questions, one minute, one tap per answer: time spent checking, how often a shipment had to change last month, cost of the last incident, a fair price, and whether they would share what their drivers see. It feeds four of the thresholds above, so the eight interviews are not the only numbers. Target: 30 answers from offices with lanes abroad. Offices answer about their own desk; people who know the field (professors, consultants, former staff) answer the same topics from experience, and the two are counted apart. Open the survey
