Code where the rules are known, AI where the text is unstructured, a person in between

  • 7 countries of ban rules in code, plus the official Tyrol dosing calendar
  • AI reads notices and news; one person approves every event before it goes out
  • 1 WhatsApp message per office per evening, with each option priced

1Watch

National truck bans for 7 countries as rules, the official Tyrol dosing calendar, EU traffic data feeds (DATEX II), news and union releases.

2Verify

Claude turns text into a structured event with a verbatim quote, checked against the source. A person approves it; review time is measured.

3Match

Each load is driven along its corridor: arrival at every pass, tunnel, border and city, EU driving-time rules, ferries, Swiss customs, exemptions.

4Alert

Leave earlier, leave later, change pass or hold, each with its cost. A WhatsApp template outside the 24-hour window; replies 1, 2, 0.
