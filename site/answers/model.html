Question 3Clear business model

A subscription per vehicle: every office on the same roads shares one set of checked events

  • €12 a vehicle a month, after a free 4-week pilot; the first 10 offices pay €8
  • 15 paying offices by the end of 2027: our target for the first 12 months
  • €0 raised so far: the first round is sized after the pilots

One Brenner closure is read, checked and encoded once, then matched against the loads of every office that uses that pass. That keeps the price low for each office.

Winning an office

About €600 in visits and calls, paid back in 2.4 months. An assumption until the pilots.

What moves the plan

The price and the cost to win an office. That is why the interviews test four prices first.
