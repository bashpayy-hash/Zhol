Question 3Clear business model

A subscription per vehicle, on costs that every office on the same roads shares

  • 92% gross margin at 100 offices: every office on the same roads shares one set of events
  • €155k of funding to break even, in May 2029
  • €771k a year of revenue in year 3, from €35k in year 1

One Brenner closure is read, checked and encoded once, then matched against the loads of every office that uses that pass. The tenth office costs almost nothing to add.
