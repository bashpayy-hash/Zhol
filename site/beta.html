How the beta works

Four weeks, five steps

  1. 1

    You send your plan. The shipments of the coming days, as Excel, CSV or a photo of the plan: reference, from–to, departure time, cargo type and vehicle. No driver names.

  2. 2

    zhol estimates every shipment. Will a ban, a dosing day, a tunnel or pass closure, a strike or a blockade hit it, how many hours it would lose and what that costs. As soon as the plan arrives and again whenever something changes; the full check runs every day at 17:30 and 20:00.

  3. 3

    One WhatsApp, only when it matters. If a shipment is at risk, one message with the options (leave earlier, later, by another crossing, or hold) and what each costs. If things change, a strike called off or a pass closed, we write again. If nothing is wrong, we do not write.

  4. 4

    A person checks first. Someone in our team checks every event before it reaches you, and every message names its source.

  5. 5

    A report every Friday. The alerts sent, which were useful and which were wrong, what we missed, and the saving estimated with the cost figures agreed with you.

From you

Your plan each day, and a word when an alert was wrong.

Agreed on day one

A success criterion, for example at least 3 useful alerts and €500 of verified savings in four weeks, with fewer than 1 wrong alert in 5.

After four weeks

If it worked: the founding-customer price, €8 a vehicle a month for 12 months, then €12. If not: no commitment. Cancel any time.
