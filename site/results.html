What 11 people told us about freight that stops

9 people who know the field from teaching, research or work around transport, and 2 small hauliers, answered our one-minute survey. They answered from experience, about traffic offices in general. These are signals, not proof. The offices themselves are what we test next.

What we changed because of it

  • Nothing to install. An office loads its week from Excel, CSV or by hand, no software to connect.
  • Trust is earned in the open. Every event shows its source, what our AI agents read is marked as not checked by a person, and we keep score of our own forecasts.
  • Useful before we ask. Each company gets its own risk report on its lanes, each association a bulletin of its corridor, before any request.

Who answered

  • 9 people who know the field. 5 teach or research logistics and 4 work around it (forwarding, associations, consulting). The part of transport they know best is shippers (3), rail and intermodal (3), several (2) and ferries and ports (1). Nobody from road haulage answered, so the offices are the gap we close next.
  • 2 small hauliers. One with 1–5 vehicles, the other with 6–20 vehicles, without trips abroad, so outside the offices we build for first. Their answers are kept apart and judge nothing.
  • 7 of the 11 answers came through Bocconi professors, who shared the survey with their network. Thank you.

What we still do not know

Whether traffic offices themselves agree. We fixed our thresholds before collecting (offices, not experts), and we test them in interviews and free four-week pilots on real loads.
