Why not just have someone check?

People check in office hours. Disruptions do not keep them.

1

Today: a person, now and then

Someone checks the news and the ban calendars between other work, in office hours. Strikes are called in the evening, closures start at 5 a.m., ferries stop at night.

2

zhol: every day, weekends too

The radar reads the news in four languages, the official calendars and the weather at the passes, and the evening message lands before the office closes.

3

Next: offices warn each other

A driver stuck at Kufstein at 4 a.m., a queue at the Messina ferries: one tap on WhatsApp, confirmed by a second report or a source, and every office with loads there knows. Anonymous. The more offices, the earlier everyone knows.
