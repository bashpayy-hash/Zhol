    What zhol does

    Estimates the risk, warns in advance, updates in real time

    Estimates

    Every shipment, in euros

    Will a ban, a closure or a strike hit it, how many hours it would lose and what that costs: the chance times the cost.

    Each event on the radar carries its chance and its cost for one trip.

    Warns in advance

    Days and weeks ahead

    As soon as an event is announced or becomes likely, with the best plan and the hour by which to decide.

    Holiday bans, dosing days and strikes are known weeks ahead.

    Updates in real time

    While it is on the way

    A strike called off, a pass closed, a ferry that did not sail: one new message, checked by a person first.

    The news is read again every 30 minutes.

    How it works for your office

    Your plan in, one message out

    1. 1

      You send your plan. The shipments of the coming days, as Excel, CSV or a photo of the plan. No driver names.

    2. 2

      zhol estimates every shipment. As soon as the plan arrives, and again whenever something changes on its route.

    3. 3

      One WhatsApp, only when it matters. The options, leave earlier, later, by another crossing or hold, and what each costs. If nothing is wrong, we do not write.

    4. 4

      You decide in a minute. The messages for the driver and the customer are ready to copy. Every event is checked by a person and names its source.

    Why not just have someone check?

    3

    Steps: a person now and then, zhol every day, then offices warning each other

    1

    Today: a person, now and then

    Someone checks the news and the ban calendars between other work, in office hours. Strikes are announced outside office hours, closures start at 5 a.m., ferries stop at night.

    2

    zhol: every day, weekends too

    The radar reads the news in four languages, the official calendars and the weather at the passes, warns as soon as something is announced and updates the alert when it changes.

    3

    Now: offices warn each other

    A driver stuck at Kufstein at 4 a.m., a strike notice from a union: tell the radar in your own words or with the document. A second office or our team confirms it, and every office with loads there knows. Anonymous. The more offices, the earlier everyone knows.
