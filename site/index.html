    Why not just have someone check?

    3

    Steps: a person now and then, zhol every day, then offices warning each other

    1

    Today: a person, now and then

    Someone checks the news and the ban calendars between other work, in office hours. Strikes are announced outside office hours, closures start at 5 a.m., ferries stop at night.

    2

    zhol: every day, weekends too

    The radar reads the news in four languages, the official calendars and the weather at the passes, warns as soon as something is announced and updates the alert when it changes.

    3

    Now: offices warn each other

    A driver stuck at Kufstein at 4 a.m., a strike notice from a union: tell the radar in your own words or with the document. A second office or our team confirms it, and every office with loads there knows. Anonymous. The more offices, the earlier everyone knows.
