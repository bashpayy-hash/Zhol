Question 2Pricing model: percentage of money saved to the company? Estimate savings

A 20-truck office saves about €1,740 a month. We charge 14% of it

The default is a subscription anchored at about 14% of the estimated saving. A pure share of savings is offered as an option.

Why not only a shareEvery invoice would become a negotiation about what the office would have known anyway, and a quiet month would bill nothing.
A verified savingCounts only alerts the dispatcher confirmed, only for events outside a published calendar, priced with the cost model the customer agreed to. The monthly statement lists every line with its source.
The €100 hourItalian law sets €100 an hour for a truck kept waiting (art. 6-bis D.Lgs. 286/2005). We price an hour lost on the road at the same rate.

Question 4A clear GTM strategy

Start where restrictions hurt most, a day trip from Milan

Who paysOwners of hauliers with 6–100 trucks and weekly lanes to DE, AT, CH, FR or PL, mostly over the Brenner. The dispatcher who plans tomorrow is the daily user.
Where firstLombardy, Veneto, Emilia-Romagna, Piemonte and Trentino-Alto Adige: about 2,350 offices. Then the South–North long haul hit by blockades, then Austrian, German and Slovenian hauliers on Italian lanes.
HowFounder-led, in Italian. A short WhatsApp or call, a 30-minute visit to review one recent disruption, then a free pilot with a written success criterion.

Question 5More details on the technical part

Code where the rules are known, AI where the text is unstructured, a person in between

1Watch

National truck bans for 7 countries as rules, the official Tyrol dosing calendar, EU traffic data feeds (DATEX II), news and union releases.

2Verify

Claude turns text into a structured event with a verbatim quote, checked against the source. A person approves it; review time is measured.

3Match

Each load is driven along its corridor: arrival at every pass, tunnel, border and city, EU driving-time rules, ferries, Swiss customs, exemptions.

4Alert

Leave earlier, leave later, change pass or hold, each with its cost. A WhatsApp template outside the 24-hour window; replies 1, 2, 0.

Question 8Is there enough space in the market for us?

Yes, for a focused wedge, with a window of two to three years

Why there is room98,277 registered hauliers, and the top 1.1% hold only a third of the fleet. Offices plan in Excel and WhatsApp; enterprise tools are priced for others.
Why nowUnplanned disruption is growing: 21 of 57 Kufstein dosing days in 2025 came outside the calendar, the Luegbrücke runs on one lane each way until 2030, and southern blockades cost €400M+ in eight days in October 2026.
The windowTwo to three years until TMS and telematics vendors add evening-before alerts. By then we want the verified event history and the association channels.
What we must earnA verified history of European road disruptions and their effect on real loads, an explainable matching engine, and trust through hauliers’ associations.
Our weak sideCalendars are free elsewhere and offices must share their plan. We count only unplanned events as value and accept the Excel they already have.
