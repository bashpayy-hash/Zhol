    Before we startWhich describes you best?

    If you do not plan transport yourself, the questions ask what you know of the field.

    Question 1 of 7How many vehicles do you manage (trucks, trailers, containers, wagons)?

    To compare operations of a similar size.

    Question 2 of 7How many shipments a week go abroad, by road, rail or ferry?

    Long shipments are the ones most exposed to bans, queues and ferries that do not sail.

    Question 3 of 7How long do you spend each day checking bans, queues and strikes?

    This is the time zhol wants to give back to you.

    Question 4 of 7In the last month, how many times did a ban, a queue, a closure or a strike make you change a shipment (time, route or date)?

    How often it happens matters as much as what it costs.

    Question 5 of 7The last time a ban, a queue or a strike stopped one of your shipments, what did it cost you?

    It tells us what an alert that arrives in time is worth.

    Question 6 of 7If other offices told you about a queue or a closure their drivers see, before your trucks leave, would you share yours too?

    Each office would have its own agent that passes these warnings to the others in seconds, anonymously.

    Question 7 of 7What price feels fair, per vehicle per month?

    To set a fair price before asking for a single euro.
