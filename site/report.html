What happens next

Nothing goes on the radar on one report alone

1

You tell it

In words, with a PDF or a photo. Anonymous: the radar never shows who sent it, and your words and documents stay with the zhol team.

2

Zholdas reads it

It finds what, where and when, matches it to the passes and routes zhol follows, and asks you if something is missing.

3

Two offices, or our check

It goes on the radar when a second office reports the same, or when our team has checked it. WhatsApp alerts go out only after a person has checked.

How sure the radar is: two independent offices 65%, three or more 85%; checked by our team 80%; with an official document or a source 90%. Like every chance on the radar, these are checked after the date and corrected.
