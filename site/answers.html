Why not only a percentage of savings

Every invoice would become a negotiation about what the office would have known anyway, and a quiet month would bill nothing. So the default is a subscription anchored at about 14% of savings, and the percentage is an option.

A verified saving counts only alerts the dispatcher confirmed, only for events outside a published calendar, priced with the cost model the customer agreed to. The monthly statement lists every line with its source.

Go-to-market: start where restrictions hurt most, a day trip from Milan

Who pays

Owners of hauliers with 6–100 trucks

Weekly lanes to DE, AT, CH, FR or PL, mostly over the Brenner. The dispatcher who plans tomorrow is the daily user.

Where first

Lombardy, Veneto, Emilia-Romagna, Piemonte

Then the South–North long-haul segment hit by blockades and ferries, then Austrian, German and Slovenian hauliers on Italian lanes.

How

Founder-led, in Italian

A short WhatsApp or call, a 30-minute visit to review one recent disruption, then a free pilot with a written success criterion.

Technology: code where the rules are known, AI where the text is unstructured

1

Watch

National truck bans for 7 countries as rules, the official Tyrol dosing calendar, EU traffic data feeds (DATEX II), news and union releases.

2

Verify

Claude turns text into a structured event with a verbatim quote, checked against the source. A person approves it; review time is measured.

3

Match

Each load is driven along its corridor: ETA at every pass, tunnel, border and city, EU driving-time rules, ferries, Swiss customs, exemptions.

4

Alert

Leave earlier, leave later, change pass or hold, each with its cost. WhatsApp template outside the 24-hour window; replies 1, 2, 0.

Example from the demo

Milano → München, leaving Thursday at 13:30

The truck reaches the A12 Inntal at 21:39. The winter night ban, from 20:00 Monday to Saturday, stops it until 05:00: 7 h 46 lost.

zhol suggests leaving at 11:00. The truck arrives at 21:10 the same day; estimated saving €734.

Why there is room

98,277 registered hauliers; the top 1.1% hold only a third of the fleet. Offices plan in Excel and WhatsApp; enterprise tools do not reach them.

What we must earn

A verified history of European road disruptions and their effect on real loads, an explainable matching engine, and trust through hauliers’ associations.

Our weak side

Calendars are free elsewhere and offices must share their plan. We count only unplanned events as value and accept the Excel they already have.
