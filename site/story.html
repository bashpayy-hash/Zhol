Why a calendar is not enough

At Kufstein, dosing starts at 05:00. The night ban ends at 05:00.

A truck coming from Munich may not drive the Inntal motorway before the night ban lifts at 05:00, and at that minute the dosing begins. Leaving earlier does not help; telling the customer does. zhol checks the combination, not one rule at a time.

Three people, one disruption

The owner buys, the dispatcher uses it, the forwarder needs the same answer for loads they subcontract. Each one lives it differently.

The buyer

The owner · il titolare

Their day: calls from customers asking where the goods are, and drivers asking whether to leave.

Who: on average 55 years old, running a firm with margins under 3% of revenue.

What hurts: diesel at €2.13 a litre in April 2026, about €9,000 more per truck a year; and the legal €100 an hour for waiting is almost impossible for a small firm to collect.

Pays today: telematics €10–35 per vehicle a month, a freight-exchange seat €120–200, a TMS €50–100.

We ask: what did the last disruption cost you, and who signs the cheque?

The user

The dispatcher · l’ufficio traffico

Their day: tomorrow’s plan in Excel or the TMS, then six country calendars, the Tyrol dosing PDF, two WhatsApp groups and the news, checked by hand.

What hurts: finding out at 05:00 from a driver in the queue at Kufstein what could have been known in advance.

Earns: about €28–36k gross a year in job ads; German and TIMOCOM for international desks.

What we must check: not one job ad mentions evening work or WhatsApp. When tomorrow’s plan is really final, and when news of a disruption reaches the office, is the first thing our interviews test.

We ask: when is tomorrow’s plan final, and how many minutes do you spend checking roads?

The second buyer

The forwarder · lo spedizioniere

Their day: loads placed with several hauliers, a customer who wants one answer, no visibility on the subcontractor’s route.

What hurts: the customer blames the forwarder for a closed pass the haulier knew nothing about either.

Pays today: visibility tools are paid by large shippers, not by a 20-person forwarder.

We ask: how often do you re-plan a load because of a road restriction abroad?

Dosing stays

The EU Advocate General found three Tyrolean bans unlawful but dosing compatible with EU law (16 July 2026). If the Court agrees, dosing becomes Tyrol’s main tool.

Not in any calendar

The October blockades were spontaneous, not called by the national associations: 288 protest points were mapped in the South.

Scarce drivers

About 20,000 freight drivers are missing in Italy (ANITA). Every driver-hour in a queue is capacity a haulier does not have.
