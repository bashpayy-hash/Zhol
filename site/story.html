    Why a calendar is not enough

    At Kufstein, dosing starts at 05:00. The night ban ends at 05:00.

    A truck coming from Munich may not drive the Inntal motorway before the night ban lifts at 05:00, and at that minute the dosing begins. Leaving earlier does not help; telling the customer does. zhol checks the combination, not one rule at a time.
