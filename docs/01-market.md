## 1.1 Who pays, who uses

| Role | Who | What they care about |
|---|---|---|
| **Buyer** | Owner (*titolare*) of an Italian road haulier or SME forwarder with **6–100 trucks** and **weekly lanes abroad** (DE, AT, CH, FR, PL) | Truck-days lost, penalties, customer complaints, driver overtime |
| **User** | Dispatcher in the traffic office (*addetto ufficio traffico*) who plans the loads of the next days and re-plans while they move | Not being surprised; one clear message with options; no new app |
| **Beachhead** | Lombardy, Veneto, Emilia-Romagna, Piemonte, with Brenner / Alpine lanes | The Alpine crossings are where restrictions concentrate |

## 1.3 Top-down cross-check

- European TMS market: **€1.4 bn (2024) → €2.5 bn (2029)**, +12.2% per year (Berg Insight, Aug 2025).
- Supply-chain visibility software: $3.3 bn worldwide (2025, GMI). Supply-chain risk software: ~$5 bn worldwide (2025, Fortune Business Insights).
- **Our €230 M TAM is about 16% of today's European TMS spend.** That is plausible for an add-on category
  ("disruption intelligence for carriers") that sits next to the TMS. We are not trying to replace the TMS.
- What a 30-truck firm spends on software today: roughly **€7–20 k per year (€230–670 per truck)**. That covers telematics, TMS,
  freight-exchange seats and navigation (doc 02). Our €144 per truck per year is an add-on. It has to be justified by measured savings
  (≈ €1,000 per truck per year in the reference case, doc 03).

## 1.4 Why now

| Driver | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Restrictions are growing and becoming less predictable | Kufstein 2025: **57 dosing days, 21 of them outside the published calendar** (Land Tirol; ADAC). 2026: 30 planned, plus 15 unplanned by August; 11 more planned for Q1 2027 (Land Tirol, checked 6 Nov 2026) |
| The Brenner bottleneck lasts until 2030–31 | Luegbrücke rebuild: single lane each way since 1 Jan 2025; completion end-2030 (ADAC: 2031) |

## 1.5 Is there enough space in the market for us?

**Yes, for a focused wedge. The window is roughly 2–3 years, and we have to earn the moat.**

Why there is room:

1. **No one does this job end to end.** We found no product that takes *the office's own shipments*, estimates for each one the risk and the cost of bans,
   dosing, strikes and protests, warns *in advance* and updates *in real time*, with options *on WhatsApp* (doc 02).
   Each piece exists somewhere; the combination does not.
2. **Incumbents look elsewhere.**
   - Visibility platforms (Shippeo, project44, Transporeon) are paid by shippers and are free for carriers.
   - Telematics (Webfleet, Samsara, Geotab) looks at where trucks are now, not at tomorrow's plan.
   - Risk platforms (Everstream, Prewave, Dataminr) sell to enterprise procurement at $20 k+ a year.
3. **Our customers are under-served by software.**
   - In Italy only **5% of logistics SMEs use business-intelligence tools, against 48% of large firms** (Politecnico di Milano, Oct 2026).
   - 54.7% of 500 road-freight firms do not know the e-CMR (Uniontrasporti, Dec 2025).
   - They run on phone calls, Excel and WhatsApp. That is why our product *is* WhatsApp.
4. **The market is fragmented.** 98,277 firms are registered in the Albo. Even the top 1,068 firms (1.1%) hold only 32.5% of the fleet.
   So there are thousands of mid-size offices that no enterprise sales team will visit.

What could close the space, and our answer:

| Threat | Likelihood | Our answer |
|---|---|---|
| TMS / telematics adds "restriction alerts for your loads" (Qargo, Trimble, Geotab Ace, Samsara Agent Studio) | High, within 2–3 years | Win the SMB offices they don't serve. Offer our event layer to them as an **API** (partner, not only competitor) |
| Free official data makes the calendar part a commodity (TIMOCOM ban portal, NAPs) | Certain | We already treat calendars as free. Our value is the **unplanned 30–40%** (dosing outside calendar, strikes, protests, closures) plus load matching |
| Margin pressure: > 13,000 Italian firms at risk of closure in 2026 | Medium | Outcome-linked pricing and a savings ledger that proves ROI within the first month (doc 03) |
| General AI assistants (ChatGPT Pulse, Meta Business Agent) | Medium-low | They don't have tomorrow's loads, verified sources, or accountability for a wrong alert |

## 1.6 What we still have to prove (feeds doc 06)

- The **1 in 5** share with weekly lanes abroad, and how many trucks per office are on them.
- How often an office is hit by an **unplanned** disruption (the model assumes 4% of international legs).
- Who signs the cheque (owner vs. operations manager), and the price at which they say *"too expensive"*.
