Which shipments will a restriction hit, and what will it cost?

zhol estimates for each shipment, by road, rail or sea, whether a ban, a dosing day, a closure, a strike or a protest along the route will hit it and what it costs, warns the traffic office in advance on WhatsApp with options, and updates it in real time. Here is how we answer each point of the jury’s feedback.

What the message does

Two loads go, one waits. Nobody had to read six websites.

The Sicily stoppage of 16–20 October 2026, replayed by the MVP. Pickets let fresh produce through, so the tomatoes and peppers leave. The tinned goods would lose 47 hours, so zhol holds them until the stoppage ends. The loads are invented; the event is real.

Every event keeps its source and is approved by a person before it can reach a dispatcher. Delays under 45 minutes are logged but never sent.

Pricing as a share of money saved
A 20-truck office saves about €1,740 a month. The standard price, €12 per truck, is 14% of that. A performance plan charges €49 plus 15% of verified savings, capped.
Savings ›
A clear business model
B2B subscription for traffic offices, free 4-week pilot with a savings ledger. Costs are shared across customers on the same roads, so gross margin reaches about 90% at 100 offices.

Hauliers with 6–100 trucks and Brenner lanes in Lombardy, Veneto, Emilia-Romagna and Piemonte. Founder-led Italian sales first, then associations and software partners.

A working MVP: ban rules for 7 countries, DATEX II feeds, Claude extraction with human review, trip simulation under EU driving rules, options with costs, WhatsApp delivery. 31 automated tests. Key rules re-checked at the official sources on 6 November.

Competitors, now and future
Telematics, freight exchanges, navigation, visibility, risk platforms and AI freight agents each cover part of the job. None estimates each shipment’s risk in advance and keeps it updated in real time. The biggest future threat is a TMS adding it.

Yes, for a focused wedge with a two-to-three-year window. The market is fragmented, unplanned disruption is growing, and incumbents are paid by shippers or priced for enterprises.

Go-to-market

Start where restrictions hurt most and buyers are a day trip from Milan.

Who pays

Owners of hauliers with 6–100 trucks

Weekly lanes to Germany, Austria, Switzerland, France or Poland, mostly over the Brenner. The dispatcher who plans tomorrow is the daily user.

Where first

Lombardy, Veneto, Emilia-Romagna, Piemonte

Then the South–North long-haul segment hit by blockades and ferries, then Austrian, German and Slovenian hauliers on Italian lanes.

How

Founder-led, in Italian

A short WhatsApp or call, a 30-minute visit to review one recent disruption with the dispatcher, then a free pilot with a written success criterion.

Technology

Code where the rules are known. AI only where the text is unstructured. A person approves every event.

Watch

Read every source

National ban calendars as code, DATEX II feeds from the EU national access points, Tyrol and ASFINAG calendars, news, union releases.

Verify

One format, one reviewer

Claude turns text into a structured event with a verbatim quote. A person approves it. Review time is measured.

Match

Drive each load

ETA at every pass, tunnel, border and city, with EU driving-time rules, ferries, Swiss customs and exemptions.

Alert

One message, with options

Leave earlier, leave later, change pass or hold, each with a cost. WhatsApp template outside the 24-hour window.

What the MVP already does
  • Truck-ban rules for Italy, Austria, Germany, Switzerland, France, Slovenia and Poland, including the Italian 2026 decree: bans start 4 hours later for trucks arriving from abroad and end 2 hours earlier for trucks leaving
  • 19 corridors and 18 chokepoints: Brenner, Luegbrücke, Kufstein, A12, Tarvisio, Tauern, Gotthard, San Bernardino, Fréjus, Mont Blanc, Messina strait
  • Austrian night ban with the low-noise truck exemption; A12 night ban by season; the 2026 Luegbrücke, A13 and Tauern calendars with direction of travel (checked against WKO)
  • The 41 official Kufstein dosing days for 2026 and early 2027, from the Land Tirol calendar. Next: 11, 18 and 25 November, 9 December
  • Claude extraction under a strict JSON schema, with a rule-based fallback in Italian, German, French and English
  • WhatsApp Cloud API with signed webhooks; replies 1, 2 and 0 update loads and metrics
  • Pilot metrics: precision, missed problems, review time and the savings ledger

Why there is room

98,277 registered hauliers. Only 5% of Italian logistics SMEs use business-intelligence tools, against 48% of large firms. They plan in Excel and WhatsApp.

What we must earn

A verified history of European road disruptions and their effect on real loads, an explainable matching engine, and trust through hauliers’ associations.

Our weak side

Calendars are free elsewhere, and offices must share their plan. We count only unplanned events as value, and accept the plan as the Excel they already have.
